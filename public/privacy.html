Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Rob Guest Games ("we", "us") processes, collects, and stores, how that data is used, and how you can ask us to delete it. It applies to our website, robguestgames.com, and to our games, including It's Art Restoring Time.

The short version

  • Our games do not send your personal data to us. Your saved progress and settings stay on your headset.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools in our games or on this website.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Anyone, anywhere, can ask us to delete their data for free by emailing hello@robguestgames.com.

1. Data we process, collect, or store

In our games

  • Game progress and settings. Things like your progress, choices, and preferences are saved locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data is not sent to us.
  • Headset, controller, and hand-tracking input. The game uses the position and movement of your headset, controllers, and hands while you play so you can move and interact. This input is processed on your device in real time. It is not recorded, stored, or sent to us.
  • Meta platform data. When you get our games through the Meta Horizon Store, the game may use the Meta Platform SDK to confirm that you own a valid copy (an "entitlement check"). Meta provides this check, and it may involve an app-specific user ID. We use it only to verify ownership; we do not store it on our own servers or share it. Meta handles its own data under the Meta Privacy Policy.

On this website

  • No cookies or analytics. This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.
  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • Server logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Web fonts. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information in order to deliver them.

When you contact us

  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

2. How we use data

We use the data described above only to:

  • run the game: respond to your movements, save your progress, and remember your settings;
  • confirm that you own a valid copy of the game through the Meta platform;
  • keep this website working and secure; and
  • reply to your messages and support requests.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except where the law requires it.

3. How long we keep data

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. We keep emails only as long as we need them to help you, and we delete them on request. Hosting providers keep server logs for a limited time under their own policies.

4. How to delete your data

Every user, in every country and region, can ask us to delete their data. Deletion is always free.

  • Data on your headset. Uninstalling the game from your headset deletes your saved progress and settings. Cloud backups made by Meta are managed in your Meta account settings.
  • Data we hold (such as emails you've sent us). Email hello@robguestgames.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Include the email address you contacted us from, or any other details that help us find your data. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days.
  • Data held by Meta. Data that Meta holds about your account is controlled by Meta, and you can manage or delete it through your Meta account and Meta's privacy settings.

We will fulfill every deletion request. The only exception is information we're legally required to keep (for example, to comply with a law or a valid legal request). If that ever applies, we'll tell you what we have to keep and why, and we'll delete it as soon as we're allowed to.

5. Children

Our games are not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

6. Changes to this policy

If our games or this website start handling data differently, we'll update this page and change the effective date above.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email hello@robguestgames.com.
