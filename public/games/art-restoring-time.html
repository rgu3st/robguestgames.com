It's Art Restoring Time

Restore the paintings. Rewrite the timeline.

You've been hired at the last minute to restore paintings at Tempus Exhibits: Exploring the Realm of the Possible, a small museum stocked with near-future restoration machines. It sounds like an easy night's work.

Then the paintings start to change. A peaceful landscape doesn't stay peaceful for long: every canvas in the building is sliding toward something darker. Your employer has circled the pieces they want fixed in the catalog. You can follow orders and lock in their timeline, or go rogue and decide for yourself what the world becomes.

How it plays

I.

Hands-on restoration

Use future tech like the Underpainting Monitor to see what's hidden beneath the paint and fix it.

II.

Beat the clock

One night, one ticking clock. If time runs out, the timeline decides for you.

III.

Your choices show

Whichever era wins out when you return the last painting shapes your ending, down to the fog and the set pieces around you.

IV.

Step through the rift

Didn't like how things turned out? Walk through the time rift, restart the night, and chase every ending.
