Brains of Brass: World Protocol

Crack the hash. Wake the machine. Save your people.

It's the year 3590. Your society, the 3S, survives in a city that someone else built: The Forgotten, a civilization that left almost no records behind except the AI that runs everything. Now that AI has locked up, and it wants a master password nobody knows.

The only lead points to a remarkably well-preserved Victorian warehouse once owned by Sir Charles Wheatstone. Inside is a mechanical brass computer that might be able to crack the code. With your AR visor and a wrist-mounted AI assistant, it's up to you to bring the machine back to life.

Three ways to think with your hands

I.

Route the steam

Plumb pipes to send pressure where the machine needs it.

II.

Punch the cards

Program the computer the 1800s way, one hole at a time.

III.

Build the gearbox

Assemble toothed gear trains to make the math turn.

For fans of

Classic puzzle adventures like Myst and The 7th Guest, and VR favorites like The Room VR and I Expect You To Die.
